Core Purpose & Physics Model
  • Physics PDE Constraints: Solves 2D Shallow Water Equations (Mass & Momentum Conservation) parameterized by terrain elevation z(x,y) and Manning friction coefficient n.
  • Core Outputs: Solves precise inundation depth h(t), flow velocity magnitude u, wave celerity c = √(gh), and affected valley zones.
  • Time to Peak (ETA): Calculates hydrograph travel time to downstream settlements (e.g., 14.2 min to valley floor).
